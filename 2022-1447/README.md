# Access request 2022-1447 — How the central city determines the operating budgets of three boroughs ("budget paramétrique")

**Public body:** Ville de Montréal, Service du greffe (documents retrieved by the Service des finances)
**Requester:** a resident (not the author of this repository)
**Filed:** March 23, 2022
**Answered:** July 19, 2022 (City file 2022-1447); further documents communicated during the review by the Commission d'accès à l'information, file 1028927-J (files dated November 30, 2022 to June 28, 2023)
**How this copy was obtained:** downloaded by the repository owner from the City's proactive-disclosure portal on February 18, 2025 (25 files, including the three of [`2022-1449`](../2022-1449/))

## Provenance

1. **The request (March 23, 2022).** The request letter is [`raw/O - 1 1 …`](<raw/O - 1 1 Demande 23-03-2022 Budget 3 arrondissements.pdf>). Addressed to the City's access officer, it asks for "all the documents that show how the following boroughs operational budgets are determined by the centre city for the years since 2017" for Sud-Ouest, CDN-NDG and Ville Saint-Laurent: "I understand that there is a formula used to determine the borough operational budgets. I seek documents that reveal how the formula is used and ALL the metrics and sources of this data that are used in the calculation of the operational budgets for the boroughs listed above." According to the CAI (2023 QCCAI 392, para. 2), the same requester filed a second request the same day, for all revenues collected from CDN-NDG since 2017. The part of that second request concerning documents held by the borough was transferred to the borough under s. 46 of the Act (City file 2022-1442) and answered by the borough on April 8, 2022 as borough file 2022-131; that answer is on the portal and is in [`../proactive-disclosure/budget/`](../proactive-disclosure/budget/).
2. **The City's response (July 19, 2022).** Page 1 of [`raw/O - 1 2 …`](<raw/O - 1 2 Réponse et documents 19-07-2022.pdf>) is the Service du greffe's e-mail, "Ville de Montréal - Demande 2022-1447", answering the request ("les formuales pour le budget des arrondissement Sud Ouest,, CDN-NDG, Saint Laurent Demande2021_ 1447" [*sic*]) with "une copie des documents qui ont été retracés par le Service des fiances" [*sic*], a pointer to the population decrees of the Ministère des Affaires municipales et de l'Habitation, and the notice of recourse under s. 51. It lists nine attachments: the 2014 *Réforme du financement des arrondissements* (RFA) documents of the Service des finances, which are also in `raw/` as separate files. Pages 2–258 of the same PDF are the "Budget paramétrique – Application spécifique" compilation.
3. **Review by the CAI.** The requester applied to the Commission d'accès à l'information for review. The Commission's decisions are public; they are cited here and not reproduced:
   - **2023 QCCAI 392**, file **1028927-J**, December 18, 2023 (interlocutory). The Commission found that the City had transmitted the formulas and the complete data used to calculate the three boroughs' budgets, including an Excel table prepared by a budget-planning adviser of the Service des finances to answer the request and the presentation to the DSA meeting of May 2019 on the adjustments to the formulas, but had not searched sufficiently for the source documents of those data. It ordered further searches and a sworn declaration within 30 days.
   - **2024 QCCAI 196**, file **1028927-J**, June 12, 2024 (final). After the City's sworn declaration and further documents, the Commission held that the City had made a serious search and holds no other documents; it took note of the additional documents communicated and dismissed the application ("PREND ACTE de la communication de documents additionnels au demandeur; REJETTE la demande de révision").
   - **2024 QCCAI 36**, file **1030974-J**, January 26, 2024: a related application by the same requester on revenues collected by borough. The City agreed to prepare a table of the revenues collected in each of the 19 boroughs for 2023 and sent it; the Commission ceased to examine the matter (s. 137.2).

   Hearing: September 12, 2023; member Me Martine Riendeau. The decisions are available from [SOQUIJ](https://soquij.qc.ca/) and CanLII ([2023 QCCAI 392](https://www.canlii.org/fr/qc/qccai/doc/2023/2023qccai392/2023qccai392.html), [2024 QCCAI 36](https://www.canlii.org/fr/qc/qccai/doc/2024/2024qccai36/2024qccai36.html), [2024 QCCAI 196](https://www.canlii.org/fr/qc/qccai/doc/2024/2024qccai196/2024qccai196.html)). They name the requester; this repository does not.
4. **Exhibit numbers.** Nine of the portal file names begin with a number of the form O-1-1 … O-7 ("O" for *organisme*). The numbering and the dates in the names (to June 28, 2023) indicate that the set is the City's documents as filed in the CAI review, not only the July 2022 response.
5. **The portal.** The files were downloaded from the City's proactive-disclosure portal on February 18, 2025. No version of the portal's inventory that the author has been able to consult lists 2022-1447 or 2022-1449: the copies of December 23, 2024, February 28, 2025 and October 17, 2026 in [`../proactive-disclosure/inventory/`](../proactive-disclosure/inventory/), and the Wayback Machine captures of November 28, 2023 and August 3, 2025. Depot addresses built on the pattern of other entries return 404 for both numbers (checked October 17, 2026). The source given here is therefore the portal with the download date; the files may no longer be online.
6. **Earlier publication.** 21 of these files were published by the author, without a README, in `OpenMobilityData/Parametric` on February 20, 2025 (deleted October 17, 2026, when this repository replaced it), together with a 257-page PDF made by the author from pages 2–258 of O-1-2. That split is not reproduced here; O-1-2 contains the same pages.

## Redaction by the repository owner

The requester's name and home address (O-1-1) and personal e-mail address (O-1-2, p. 1, "À :" line) were visible in the portal files. They were blacked out by the repository owner: the affected page was rasterized at 300 dpi, the lines located by OCR were covered with black boxes (the "À :" label kept), and the page was written back as an image, so that no text layer remains under the boxes. O-1-1 is a one-page file and is now an image PDF; in O-1-2 only page 1 was replaced (pages 2–258 unchanged, A3 size kept). The method is in [`../tools/`](../tools/). No other file of the set contains the requester's identifiers: every page was checked by text extraction or OCR, the spreadsheets and the PowerPoint file by byte search, and all file metadata. The PDF metadata contain City user IDs and, in O-2-9, the name of a City employee as author; they are left as released. The City's copies are not redacted.
