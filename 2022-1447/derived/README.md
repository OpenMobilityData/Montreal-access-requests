# 2022-1447 — derived work (drafts, February 2025)

**Status: unreviewed drafts.** These files were made by the author in February 2025 while reading the Excel model released under 2022-1447 ([`../raw/O - 1 3 Modèle_RFA_2017-2022_DAI_20221130.xlsx`](<../raw/O - 1 3 Modèle_RFA_2017-2022_DAI_20221130.xlsx>)). The LaTeX notes and the figures are published as they were left, for the record, and have not been checked against the release. Their figures, units and interpretations should not be cited without checking them against the spreadsheet; for example, the parameter table in `parametric_budget_model2` gives CDN-NDG an area of 85,409 hectares, which cannot be right as stated. The released documents in [`../raw/`](../raw/) are the reference.
