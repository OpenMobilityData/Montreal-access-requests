## What the table shows (figures as released, before taxes)

- **Municonseil** (five lawyers, $200–$350 an hour), two columns of invoices, April 2020 to August 2021, headed « Factures CMQ » and « Ville de Montréal & CMQ c. Sue Montgomery, 500-17-111893-205 »: subtotals 526.91 h / $143,042.26 and 362.24 h / $101,604.56; « Total des honoraires » $244,646.82 for 889.15 h; a further line « 12023 madamus », 15.76 h, $4,305.40.
- **DHC** (five lawyers, $150–$285), March 2020 to April 2021, lines marked « Injonction-CMQ » and « Sue Montgomery »: 419.38 h, $115,375.70.
- **IMK** (four lawyers, $150–$285): « Pourvoi en contrôle judiciaire et ordonnance de sauvegarde », 500-17-114125-209, invoices November 2020 to July 2021, $32,196.76 including disbursements and the court stamp; and « Recours en dommages et intérêts », 500-17-117103-211, one invoice of June 30, 2021, $315.

The three Superior Court file numbers in the table identify the proceedings: 500-17-111893-205 (the 2020 proceedings between the City and the borough mayor, decided by *Montgomery c. Ville de Montréal*, 2020 QCCS 4278), 500-17-114125-209 (the judicial review of the Commission municipale du Québec's decisions, decided by *Montgomery c. Commission municipale du Québec*, 2022 QCCS 199), and 500-17-117103-211 (the claim in damages, settled in 2023 according to press reports). The judgments are cited here and not reproduced.
